# Product

Geck Inspect is a crested-gecko-first web app for keepers and breeders: records for each gecko, breeding and lineage, passports and transfers that carry an animal's history to its buyer, a crested gecko genetics calculator, an honest photo Morph ID, reference guides, and price estimates from listing data. Live at geckinspect.com.

This file is a short summary. Updated 15 October 2026: the Morph ID brief that used to be here moved to `docs/specs/morph-id.md`.

product

## Who it is for

Crested gecko keepers (a few animals, care reminders and weights) and breeders (pairings, eggs, lineage, sales). The 90-day plan treats breeders as the paying customer to test (Part E of `docs/planning/comprehensive-review-2026-10-14.md`).

## Voice

Knowledgeable, grounded and specific, like a trusted breeder with good records. Confident when the evidence is strong, candid when it is not. Short sentences, active voice, correct crested gecko terms (Lilly White, Harlequin, Phantom, Cappuccino, Axanthic, Sable, Highway). No em dashes (see CLAUDE.md).
