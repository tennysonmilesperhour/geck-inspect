# Public domain gecko photo source manifest

The following seven source photos and file-specific rights evidence were supplied and reviewed by the photo sourcing worker. The authoritative 77-line manifest is retained in Library as `geck_demo_photo_sources.md` (`libfile_ab3b846c63a08191b86f3df75972f181`). Source files were downloaded from their listed original URLs on 2026-10-13 after a bounded per-command network approval, then visually inspected locally. All seven files are used once each on separate fictional demo records. No photo is repeated on another gecko card. The two Karen Arnold photographs may depict the same subject or session, so the records do not claim that each photograph shows a different animal.

Visual/privacy review: the macro portrait and licking-lips image are the strongest portrait crops; orange and female-2010 are used at card size without upscaling; the super-empty-back photo is dimmer; `geneva-young.jpg` is partly obscured by foliage; and `crusted-gecko.jpg` is soft/noisy. The less polished frames are retained to provide genuine image variety, and their limitations remain visible at card size. The metadata report showed camera/date fields on some files but no GPS/location fields; those camera/date fields are not used to infer demo data. Keep the visible “Illustrative photo” label and never infer the photographed subject's ownership, identity, genotype, age, sex, breeding, health, sales, or results from the image. The Vsnary source identifies Terrarium Station LLC as breeder; this does not indicate user ownership. The two Karen Arnold images may show the same subject/session.

All photos, when installed, must be labeled: “Illustrative photo.
