# Public domain gecko photo source manifest

The following seven source photos and file-specific rights evidence were supplied and reviewed by the photo sourcing worker. The authoritative 77-line manifest is retained in Library as `geck_demo_photo_sources.md` (`libfile_ab3b846c63a08191b86f3df75972f181`). Source files were downloaded from their listed original URLs on 2026-10-13 after a bounded per-command network approval, then visually inspected locally. The selected demo records use four photos: `macro-portrait.jpg`, `licking-lips.jpg`, `orange.png`, and `super-empty-back.jpg`. The other three are retained with attribution for optional later use, not assigned to demo animals.

Visual/privacy review: the macro portrait and licking-lips image are the strongest portrait crops; orange is clear at its native small dimensions; the super-empty-back photo is dimmer and used only as a smaller card image. `geneva-young.jpg` is partly obscured by foliage, `crusted-gecko.jpg` is soft/noisy, and `female-2010.jpg` depicts a gecko held in a hand, so these are not assigned to demo records. The metadata report showed camera/date fields on some files but no GPS/location fields; those camera/date fields are not used to infer demo data. Keep the visible “Illustrative photo” label and never infer the photographed subject's ownership, identity, genotype, age, sex, breeding, health, sales, or results from the image. The Vsnary source identifies Terrarium Station LLC as breeder; this does not indicate user ownership. The two Karen Arnold images may show the same subject/session.

All photos, when installed, must be labeled: “Illustrative photo.
