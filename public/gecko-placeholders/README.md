# Illustrated gecko defaults

Five fixed artwork families: botanical engraving, indigo woodcut, Art Nouveau, celestial enamel and terracotta print. Each family has hatchling, juvenile and adult illustrations, exported as 512px and 1024px square WebP files. The full frame is always contained in its image slot.

Created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on October 14, 2026. These are decorative crested gecko placeholders, not specimens or morph identification examples. Source artwork reserves blank cartouches for live application labels.

The application selects a family from the saved gecko ID. Editing the name, sex or hatch date does not shuffle that family. Do not reorder or extend the family list without migrating that mapping. Recorded male, female and unsexed values use a symbol, word and family-specific accent. Age artwork follows the saved hatch date: under 6 months, 6 to 17 months, and 18 months onward. These visual bands do not determine breeding readiness. Missing or ambiguous dates remain explicitly unknown. Real photos always take priority.

Preview all combinations at the development-only /DesignPreview#placeholders route. Full generation prompts and original PNGs are preserved in the task's outputs/gecko-placeholder-families-2026-10-14 folder.
