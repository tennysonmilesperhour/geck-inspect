# Guest demo photographs

Real photographs from the founder’s already-public Geck Inspect records, reused for the owner-requested homepage tour and demo collection. These are owner-authorized site assets, not stock photos represented as public domain. Images are resized with EXIF orientation applied and metadata removed. No generated imagery or changes to animal colors or patterns.

## Morph audit, October 14, 2026

Each photo was checked visually against its source record. `src/data/demo-gecko-portraits.js` stores the reviewed photo and conservative trait labels together. Both the original collection and the additional breeder ledger use that registry. A record cannot select a generic photo independently of its morph labels.

Names, sexes, ages, weights, sales, care histories, and family relationships in the demo are fictional. The portrait illustrates the listed morph, not the identity or pedigree of that sample animal. Lilly White examples follow the source keeper’s identification as well as visible white distribution. A photograph alone cannot prove a genotype or recessive carrier status.
