When a crested gecko changes hands, its history should go with it. A careful
buyer asks for the hatch date, the current weight and a photo of the parents.

A passport gives a gecko a public page that shows the lineage and weigh-ins
you have logged, plus a QR card that links to it. Making one turns the gecko's
Public display on, so the link works. Make it before the sale, not after.

The Buyer packet puts the record in one PDF: photo, traits, lineage, weights,
feeding and the passport QR. Hand it over with the gecko. Once the sale is
done, use Transfer to a new owner so the buyer can claim the record.

Timing matters as much as paperwork. Let a young gecko go only once it eats
reliably and has had a good shed.

To start, open the gecko, go to Passport and papers and choose Make a
passport.
