Crested geckos do best at room temperature. Days of 72 to 78°F suit them, with
a slight drop at night to 65 to 72°F.

The number to remember is 82°F. Above it, a crested gecko is under acute heat
stress, and staying there is fatal. Overheating is a far more common killer
than cold, and one of the most common ways keepers lose a crested gecko.

Keep a digital thermometer at the warmest and the coolest spot in the
enclosure, and check both readings at least once a week. Check again whenever
your room warms up, and keep the enclosure away from windows.

Most homes never need extra heat, and a cooler night is normal. If your home
runs below 68°F, use only a low-wattage deep heat projector on a thermostat.
Never use a heat bulb or heat pad without one.
