A falling weight is often the first sign of a problem in a crested gecko,
earlier than a change in appetite. Weigh-ins only help when they happen on
time, so let Geck Inspect keep the rhythm for you.

Each gecko's record has a weigh-in reminder switch. It follows the gecko's
life stage: every 14 days for a hatchling or juvenile, every 21 days for a
sub-adult and every 30 days for an adult. Those are the longest gaps to leave.
Weigh a new or struggling hatchling every week.

New geckos get the reminder on by default. Older records start with it off, as
do any you switched off.

To check, open each gecko in My Geckos and look under Weights for the Weigh-in
reminder switch. The Weigh-in Reminders switch in Settings covers your whole
account, so leave that on too.

Once a reminder is on, its weigh-ins appear on the Today screen when due, next
to feedings, eggs close to hatching and vet follow-ups. Today opens even with
no signal.

No gecko yet? Add one first, and its reminder starts on.
