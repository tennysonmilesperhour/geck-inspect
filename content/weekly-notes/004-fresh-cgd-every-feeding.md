Crested gecko diet, or CGD, is a complete diet on its own. It is made to be a
crested gecko's only food. Because it is the whole diet, keeping it fresh
matters.

Mix 1 part powder to 2 parts water until it has the texture of ketchup. Make
it fresh at each feeding instead of keeping a batch in the fridge, and serve
it in a flat, shallow dish.

Throw it out after 24 to 36 hours, even if your gecko has not touched it. Old
CGD breeds yeast and fruit flies.

Check the manufacture date on the pouch too. CGD loses vitamin potency after
about 12 months, so small pouches bought often stay fresher than one big bag.
Over your gecko's life, rotate between at least two brands.

Log each feeding in Geck Inspect. With feeding reminders on, Today shows when
each gecko's next feeding is due, so the schedule is not kept in your head.
