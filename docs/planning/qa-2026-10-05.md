# Real-browser QA, 5 October 2026

The features shipped on 2 and 3 October had never been clicked through in a real browser. This pass drove the app in Chromium (Playwright) at a phone size (390 x 844, touch on) and a desktop size (1440 x 900), on the public pages and through the guest demo. Screenshots are in [qa-2026-10-05/](qa-2026-10-05/) (32 files, about 1.3 MB). The code fixes are in commit `ed3399e` ("QA fixes from the 5 Oct real-browser pass").

## 1. How it was tested, and what could not be

- **Where it ran.** The local dev server (`vite`, with `.env.local` holding the public Supabase URL and anon key, not committed) and, for load timing, a production build served by `vite preview`.
- **Production was not reachable.** The sandbox's network policy refused connections to geckinspect.com and to the Supabase project. So every database call was answered inside the browser instead: public reads got either empty results or small made-up records (a passport gecko called "Sable Moon", a waitlist and a store page, all fake), and the landing counts used the real totals (56 keepers, 349 geckos) read once with a SELECT. Nothing was written anywhere, no account was created and no form was submitted to production.
- **What that means for the results.** Layout, navigation, the demo, console errors, crashes and dead links are real findings. Anything that depends on live data (real listings, real forum posts, real photos, live Morph ID) still needs a look on a phone against geckinspect.com. Photos hosted elsewhere (Wikimedia demo photos, the Supabase photo store, geckinspect.com/logo.png) were blocked here, so broken photos in the screenshots are the sandbox, not the app, unless a finding says otherwise.
- **Pages covered (both sizes).** Landing page and every page it links to: Morph Guide index, a morph page (Lilly White), the category and inheritance hubs, Care Guide, a care topic, the care series, Genetics Guide, the calculator plus Reverse, Clutch Lab and a per-morph calculator page, Membership, Morph ID front door, sign in, sign up and forgot password, About, Contact, Quality Scale, Verification, the pedigree, breeding records and price guide pages, Privacy, Terms, the blog index (only to check it loads, see the note in section 5), Store, an unknown address, plus 80 more internal links found on those pages. Passport, passport QR, waitlist, store page and a breeder page were checked with the made-up records.
- **Guest demo (entered with "Try the demo first").** The three-stop guided tour end to end; Dashboard, My Geckos, the gecko record window, Add Gecko, Breeding, the Hatchery tab, the hatch dialog and its save, Field Mode and its log screen, Market, Forum, Notifications, Messages, Settings, Season Planner, Printable Worksheets and the rack label sheet, Lineage, Batch Husbandry, Pairing Planner, My Store, My Listings, Market Pricing, Evidence Lab, Image Import, Seller Console, MorphMarket export, Morph Guide submissions, AI Consultant, Gallery, Other Reptiles, Breeding Season.
- **Checks on every page.** Console errors and crashes, failed requests to the app itself, sideways scrolling on the phone, broken images, cut-off text, small tap targets, "page not found" or sign-in walls where content was expected, and the time to the largest piece of content appearing (LCP, a standard speed measure).

**Overall:** no page crashed, no page scrolled sideways on a phone, and no app file failed to load. The problems are in the demo (the main way a visitor tries the app before signing up), in a few places public visitors hit a sign-in wall, and in phone polish.

## 3. What worked well

- No crashes, no sideways scrolling and no failed app files on any of the 36 public pages, 80 linked pages or 32 demo screens.
- The demo tour runs end to end on both sizes (collection, Nimbus's record, the calculator with his parents loaded, then the sign-up card) and the closing card is clear.
- The hatch dialog, Field Mode's big buttons, the [rack label sheet picker](qa-2026-10-05/m-i-rack-labels-2.jpg) and the record window all lay out well on a phone. The demo Dashboard ([phone](qa-2026-10-05/m-g-dashboard.jpg), [desktop](qa-2026-10-05/d-g-dashboard.jpg)) and the [AI Consultant](qa-2026-10-05/m-g-consultant.jpg) load cleanly. The guest "view-only" message appears exactly where a save is attempted.
- Passport, [waitlist](qa-2026-10-05/m-waitlist.jpg) (odds, deposit terms) and [store pages](qa-2026-10-05/d-storepage.jpg) render cleanly with sample data, with no member email anywhere in the page.
- Public guides, [calculator pages](qa-2026-10-05/m-calculator.jpg), [Membership](qa-2026-10-05/m-membership.jpg) and the [Morph ID front door](qa-2026-10-05/m-recognition.jpg) have one clear heading each and readable text at phone size.

## 5. What is left, in suggested order

1. **Let the demo show prices (item 2).** Give guest mode a sample Market brief and price estimates on the demo geckos, labelled as sample data. This is the landing page's lead promise and the demo currently skips it.
2. **Make "Add Gecko" in the demo convert (item 3).** Open Quick Add with the demo's data, and on save send the visitor to sign-up with "Create a free account to keep this gecko" and a way back. Then count demo-to-sign-up in PostHog.
3. **Shrink the demo tour card on phones (item 6)** to a one-line bar with "Next", and keep the floating notices (tour, feeding reminders, guest banner) from stacking in the same corner (item 5).
4. **Add the landing screenshots (item 7).** Now that the demo's Hatchery works, the eight screenshots listed in `public/screenshots/README.md` can be captured from the demo on a wide screen.
5. **Split the main script (item 8)** so the landing page does not carry the Dashboard and profile code, then re-measure on a real phone against geckinspect.com.
6. **Give the Gallery link and unknown addresses a real answer (item 10):** either open the Gallery to signed-out visitors (it is community content, good for search) or show a short sign-in note; and show "page not found" for addresses that match nothing.
7. Items 14 to 17, then a guest-mode pass over the screens in item 16.
8. **Repeat this pass on production** from a network that can reach geckinspect.com, on a real iPhone and Android phone, with live Market data, real photos and Morph ID. That is the check this sandbox could not do.
